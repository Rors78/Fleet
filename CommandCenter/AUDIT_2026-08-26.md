# Fleet Audit — 2026-08-26

**Scope:** the fleet's published performance figures, as an investor's analyst would
check them. Every number below is re-derived from the durable event bus
(`logs/event_bus/*.jsonl`, `logs/events/*.jsonl`, 52 files) and live endpoints, not
from the dashboard or from any previous session's notes.

**Bottom line: the fleet's reported profit does not survive audit. Do not show the
current numbers to an investor as a track record.** The system has a real skeleton —
18 live services, a working event bus, an expectancy engine that is *more* honest than
the display — but the P/L it reports is an artifact of position sizes that were never
possible and synthetic test rows that were never trades.

---

## 1. The headline number is fabricated three ways over

Dashboard header, live: `WR: 66.7% (n=66)` and a fleet P/L of `+$736`.

### 1a. $1,135 of the profit is synthetic test probes

TurtleSue reports 97 closes, 92 wins, 1 loss — a 98.9% win rate. Every one of those
92 wins looks like this:

```
pair=ZZPROBE032265B/USD   pnl=12.34  entry=4.1  exit=4.21
pair=ZZPROBE032309B/USD   pnl=12.34  entry=4.1  exit=4.21
pair=ZZPROBE033089B/USD   pnl=12.34  entry=4.1  exit=4.21
```

Identical P/L, identical entry, identical exit, `ZZPROBE` pair names. These are
hardcoded test fixtures, not trades.

| | closes | probes | real | probe P/L | real P/L |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| turtlesue | 97 | **92** | 5 | **+1135.28** | **−0.00** |
| gridzilla | 43 | 0 | 43 | 0.00 | +1451.41 |
| confluence | 19 | 0 | 19 | 0.00 | −224.73 |

**TurtleSue's real trading P/L is $0.00 across 5 trades.** Its entire contribution to
the fleet's profit is fake.

### 1b. The profit was earned on a $1M pool and is displayed against a $212 pool

Recorded position sizes look impossible against today's pool of **$212.48**:

| bot | n | min size | median | max size | max ÷ today's pool |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| gridzilla | 189 | $7,165.66 | $10,658.72 | $49,784.29 | 234× |
| confluence | 19 | $3.41 | $11.70 | $66,710.60 | 314× |

**197 of 208 positions exceed the entire current pool.** My first reading was that
the sizing guard was broken. It is not — `PortfolioManager.reserve()`
(`command_center.py:512`) enforces fleet, per-bot, per-pair, directional and
per-trade caps, all against `self.total`, and fails ARMED when state is unreadable.
It is a genuinely careful piece of code.

The real explanation is an **era boundary**. Reservation sizes by day:

```

2026-08-07    120 events   median    600.00   max 66,710.60
2026-08-10     26 events   median 43,396.22   max 48,715.32

2026-08-17      4 events   median      5.47   max     11.70
2026-08-26      1 event    median     13.10   max     13.10
```

Those $40–66k positions were **correct** against the $1M pool that existed then.
From 2026-08-16 the fleet sizes correctly at $5–13. Nothing is over-leveraged today.

**The defect is that P/L earned on a $1M pool is displayed beside today's $212 pool
as though the two were commensurable.** Splitting real (non-probe) trades at the

**Every dollar of Gridzilla's +$1,451 was earned in the $1M era. It has not closed a
single trade since the pool was cut** — which is exactly why the panel reads
`cycles: 0` and `"No completed grids yet"` beside a `+$913` P/L.

In the current configuration the fleet's entire record is **8 trades and +$1.86**.

### 1c. The header rate is a mean-of-rates, and its n is not its denominator

`66.666...%` is exactly **(100 + 100 + 0) / 3** — an unweighted mean of three bots'
win rates. It weights a 16-trade bot the same as a 19-trade bot and the same as a bot
with zero decided trades.

`(n=66)` is a **sum of trade counts** printed beside a rate whose actual denominator
is **3**. It matches neither total trades (57) nor decided trades (35).

The correct pooled figure — which `/api/expectancy` already computes — is
**65.7% over n=35**.

## 2. Gridzilla's 100% is structural: losing grids are never recorded

**This is the single most important finding in the audit, and it outranks every
display defect below.**

> **FIXED 2026-08-26 (`ad1a81e`).** `remove_grid` now records through
> `expectancy.record_trade` using `total_pnl_mtm` (realized + unrealized on
> abandoned inventory) instead of `grid_pnl`. The double-count guard is preserved:
> a teardown with zero completed cycles records the full mark-to-market, one with
> completed cycles records only the unrealized remainder. Guarded by
> `tests/test_gridzilla_records_losses.py`, proven red-capable against the
> committed original.
>
> **The 155 historical losses cannot be recovered** — the `GRID_KILLED` payload
> only ever carried `grid_pnl`, never the mark-to-market, so their true P/L was
> never written down. The fix measures correctly going forward; it does not
> repair the past. **Gridzilla's pre-fix ledger should be treated as unmeasured,
> not retroactively corrected.**

Gridzilla has **never recorded a single loss** — 16W / 0L / 17F across 33 records.
That is not a hot streak. It is unreachable-by-a-loss by construction:

- All 33 expectancy records come from **one** call site: the completed-cycle path
  in `check_fills` (`gridzilla.py:972`).
- Losing grids exit through `remove_grid` (`gridzilla.py:1214`), which appends to a
  local `trade_history` list and **never calls `expectancy.record_trade`**.

Verified independently against the durable bus:

```
GRID_KILLED events : 167
  negative P/L     :   0
  positive P/L     :  13
  zero P/L         : 154
```

**167 grids killed, not one booked a loss.** A volatility grid engine that has torn
down 167 grids without a single negative outcome is not winning — it is only
counting its winners.

The consequence: **Gridzilla's expectancy is not a measurement of profitability at
all.** Its win rate cannot fall below 100% no matter what the market does, and its
+$1,451 is a sum over a population that excludes every loss by design. No display
fix repairs this; the recording path has to change.

From the bot's own `/api/snapshot`:

```

win_rate: 100.0    max_consecutive_losses: 17
```

Three defects in one payload:

## 5. The system is better than its numbers

Worth saying, because it is the case for fixing rather than abandoning:

- **`/api/expectancy` is already honest.** It reports `flat: 17`, `n_decided: 16`, a
  95% CI of `[80.6, 100.0]`, and correctly marks TurtleSue `UNMEASURED` with
  `expectancy: null`. The backend knows what it doesn't know; the *display* discards it.
- **An `amnesiaGuard()` helper already exists** (built in a 2026-07-29 audit) for
  exactly the restart-amnesia case. Gridzilla calls it for NET PNL — the win-rate card
  walks straight past it.
- 18 services live, event bus working, expectancy engine with CIs and verdicts.

The infrastructure is real. The measurement layer is real. What failed is that
nothing forced the display to inherit the backend's honesty, and nothing forced
recorded trades to be consistent with the pool that funded them.

## 6. Recommended order of work

0. **Record losing grids.** `remove_grid` must call `expectancy.record_trade` for
   torn-down grids, or Gridzilla's win rate is permanently pinned at 100% by
   construction. Until this lands, **no Gridzilla figure means anything** — this
   outranks every item below.
1. **Filter `ZZPROBE` rows out of `expectancy.py`.** The canonical predicate already
   exists in `command_center.py:3504`, `signal_broadcaster.py:701` and
   `weekly_analysis.py:166`, with tests. `expectancy.py` — the one that feeds the
   dashboard — is the sibling that was missed. This exact bug already bit once
   (2026-08-07: *"50 of 73 rows were probes contributing +$617 of fabricated P/L,
   flipping the raw sum positive"*).
2. **Make the $1M/$212 era boundary structural, not cosmetic.** Any P/L, win rate or
   expectancy figure that spans 2026-08-13 must either be split or labelled. Today
   the two eras are summed into one number. The cleanest fix is to stamp each trade
   with the pool size in force at the time and refuse to aggregate across differing
   values without an explicit flag.
3. **Make P/L derivable.** Require entry, exit, size and direction on every close and
   assert `pnl ≈ (exit−entry)/entry × size × dir` at write time. 11 of 11 checkable
   Gridzilla trades currently fail this.
4. **Fix TRADE_OPEN emission** (Confluence emits none; Gridzilla loses 103). Without
   opens, no third party can replay the book.
5. **Pooled win rate everywhere**, with the true denominator as the displayed n, and
   surface the CI the backend already computes.
6. **Never render an unmeasurable rate in confident green.** Neutral grey exists in
   the code for exactly this and is unused here.

Until 1–4 land, **the fleet has no publishable track record** — not because it lost
money, but because its wins cannot be reconstructed from its own records.
